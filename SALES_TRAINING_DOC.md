# Sales Training: Selling Our Custom Website & CRM Solution

**Target Audience:** Non-technical Sales Team  
**Goal:** Explain *how* we build websites and *why* it benefits the client, without getting bogged down in code.

---

## 1. The "Elevator Pitch"

**What do we sell?**  
We don't just build "brochure websites." We build **high-performance digital platforms** that come with a **built-in engine for running their business**.

**The Hook:**  
*"Most agencies will build you a website and walk away. We build you a website that actually captures leads and helps you manage them in a private dashboard, without you needing to pay for expensive software like HubSpot or Salesforce."*

---

## 2. Our Technology (The "Special Sauce")

You don't need to know how to code, but you do need to know the brand names we use and why they matter.

### A. The Frontend: **Next.js** (The "Showroom")
Think of this as the part of the car everyone sees. 
*   **What it is:** The modern standard for the web (used by Netflix, TikTok, Nike).
*   **Why clients care:** 
    *   **Speed:** It loads instantly. Google loves fast sites, which helps with SEO (ranking higher in search results).
    *   **Mobile-First:** It looks perfect on phones, tablets, and desktops automatically.

### B. The Backend: **Supabase** (The "Vault")
Think of this as the engine and the trunk where data is stored.
*   **What it is:** A secure database and authentication system.
*   **Why clients care:** 
    *   **Security:** Their customer data is encrypted and safe.
    *   **Real-time:** When a customer fills out a form, it appears in their admin panel *instantly*. No delays.

---

## 3. The "Killer Feature": The In-Built CRM

This is our massive competitive advantage. Most competitors build a Wordpress site and tell the client to "check their email" for leads.

**What we provide:**
Every website we ship comes with a secure **`/admin` portal**. 

1.  **Lead Capture:** When a visitor fills out the "Contact Us" form, the data doesn't just go to email. It is saved directly into the website's database.
2.  **Kanban Board:** We include a drag-and-drop board (like Trello) inside their website admin area.
    *   *New Lead* -> *Contacted* -> *Negotiation* -> *Closed*.
3.  **Ownership:** The client owns this data. They aren't renting it from a SaaS company that charges $50/user/month.

## 5. Typical Client Objections (Scripts)

**Client:** *"Why not just use WordPress? My nephew knows WordPress."*

We build with Next.js—the same technology big tech companies use—so your site is faster, more secure, and comes with business tools built right into the core, not taped on."*

**Client:** *"I already have a CRM."*
**You:** *"That's great! We can easily integrate with your existing tools if you prefer. However, our solution ensures that you have a backup of all your leads directly on your own server, giving you 100% data ownership."*
